# Apps by industry

Which embeddable apps fit each lane in the gallery. Template structure is the
lane's own decision; these apps slot into any of its pages via the pipeline's
--apps flag. Ordering is roughly by impact on leads.

## Home services (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, landscaping)
quote-calculator, service-request, before-after, payment-estimator,
hours-status, faq, review-wall, location-card

## Restaurants
menu, reservation, gift-card, promo-banner, hours-status, location-card,
photo-gallery, review-wall, testimonial-carousel

## Salons and barbershops
booking-request, before-after, team-grid, gift-card, photo-gallery,
promo-banner, review-wall

## Law offices
faq, service-request, team-grid, testimonial-carousel, hours-status
